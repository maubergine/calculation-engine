About Pension Annual Allowance

The pension annual allowance is the maximum amount of pension savings you can make each year with tax relief.

Tapering for High Earners

Your annual allowance may be reduced (tapered) if both:

  • Your 'threshold income' is over £200,000
  • Your 'adjusted income' is over £240,000

For every £2 your adjusted income exceeds £240,000, your annual allowance is reduced by £1, down to a minimum of £10,000.

Key Terms
  • Adjusted income: Your total income plus the value of any employer pension contributions
  • Threshold income: Your total income minus personal pension contributions
