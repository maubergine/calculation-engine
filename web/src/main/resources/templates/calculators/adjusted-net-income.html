About Adjusted Net Income

Adjusted net income is used to calculate several aspects of your tax situation, including:

  • The reduction in your personal allowance if your income exceeds £100,000
  • The High Income Child Benefit Charge if your income exceeds £50,000
  • Eligibility for some tax reliefs and allowances

Adjusted net income is your total taxable income (before personal allowances) minus specific deductions:

  • Pension contributions paid gross or with tax relief at source (the grossed-up amount)
  • Gift Aid donations (the grossed-up amount)
  • Trading losses (where applicable)

Note: This calculator provides an estimate based on the information you enter and should not be relied upon for tax filing purposes. Always consult a tax professional for tax advice.
