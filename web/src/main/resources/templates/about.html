About UK Tax Calculator

Purpose

The UK Tax Calculator is designed to help individuals estimate their UK tax liabilities based on their income, dividends, pensions, and other financial details. It's built on a comprehensive calculation engine that includes the latest tax bands and allowances for different financial years.

Features

  • Income tax calculations for employment income
  • Dividend tax calculations
  • Personal allowance tapering for high earners
  • Pension contribution tax relief
  • Support for multiple tax years
  • Detailed tax breakdown by rates

Calculation Engine

The calculator is powered by a sophisticated calculation engine built in Java. The engine employs a modular architecture with specialized calculators for different aspects of taxation, ensuring accurate and comprehensive tax calculations.

Key components include:

  • Income tax calculator with band-specific rates
  • Personal allowance calculator with income-based tapering
  • Pension contribution calculator with tax relief
  • Dividend tax calculator with appropriate allowances
